## Behaviour (decided by planner)
Every entry has `lang: "zh"`, `kind: "meme"`, the exact `term` below, `pinyin` (syllables separated by spaces, tone marks, English letters kept as written), a literal translation in `literal`, a one-sentence `meaning` in English, an `origin`, a `register`, and `equivalent` with `en` and `es`. Use only the sense and year given here; do not add facts that are not in this list. When the list says `origin unclear`, the `origin` field must be exactly `Origin unclear; spread on Chinese social media around <year>.` with the year filled in. If the linter flags a term as ambiguous (short or ordinary word), set `"ambiguous": true` as the linter requires.
The 12 terms (term: sense; year):
1. 孔乙己文学: self-mocking posts by graduates who feel stuck in their degree pride, comparing themselves to Kong Yiji, Lu Xun's character who cannot take off his scholar's long gown; 2023.
2. 脆皮大学生: "brittle-skin students": young people who get hurt or ill from ordinary things (a sneeze, a stretch); 2023.
3. 偷感: a "sneaky feel": acting furtive and shy, as if hiding, as in 偷感很重; 2024. Origin unclear.
4. 淡人: a low-desire, calm, unbothered person who does not compete or seek attention; 2023.
5. 多巴胺穿搭: "dopamine dressing": bright, colourful outfits worn to lift your mood; 2023.
6. 嘴替: "mouth substitute": a person or comment that says what you wanted to say; 2020.
7. 反内卷: resisting involution (the pointless rat race, 内卷); 2021.
8. 科目三: literally "driving test subject three"; since 2023 also a viral dance associated with that name; 2023.
9. 泰酷辣: a cutesy, mock-foreign respelling of 太酷啦, "so cool!"; 2023. Origin unclear.
10. 芭比Q: from "BBQ": "I'm grilled", meaning "I'm done for / it's ruined"; 2022.
11. 命运的齿轮开始转动: "the gears of fate begin to turn": an ironic, over-dramatic line said when something small starts a chain of events; 2022. Origin unclear.
12. city不city: "city or not city?": is it urban, trendy and stylish or not; mixes English and Chinese; 2023. Origin unclear.
